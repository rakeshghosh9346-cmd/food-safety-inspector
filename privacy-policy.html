Privacy Policy for SafeBite Food Safety Inspector

Last updated: October 2026

SafeBite Food Safety Inspector ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the SafeBite Food Safety Inspector Android application.

1. Information We May Access

SafeBite may request access to your device camera when you choose to take or select a food sample image for inspection.

The app may also process information necessary to provide food quality and safety inspection results through its connected web-based service.

2. Camera Permission

The application may request camera permission to allow users to capture food sample photographs. Camera access is used only when the user chooses to use the food inspection feature.

3. Photos and Images

Images selected or captured for food inspection may be transmitted to the connected food inspection service for processing and generating inspection results. Users should avoid uploading photographs containing unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed by the application may be used to:

  • Perform food quality and safety inspection.
  • Generate inspection results and confidence information.
  • Provide food safety warnings.
  • Generate digital inspection or compliance reports.
  • Improve the functionality and reliability of the application.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties. Information may be processed by services required to operate the application's food inspection functionality.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the application. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

SafeBite Food Safety Inspector is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party technologies and web services to provide its functionality. Such services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or SafeBite Food Safety Inspector, you may contact the development team.
